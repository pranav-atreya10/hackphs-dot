Why it matters

The sky over a runway is crowded

Birds and drones share the same low airspace that every departing aircraft climbs through. Big airports pay for radar to watch it. Most small airports watch it by eye.

How it works

It sees, it hears, it speaks up

One phone on a tripod at the runway edge does the watching. A second laptop in the tower shows and speaks the alerts.

Sees birds

A ready-made AI model runs in the phone’s browser and boxes every bird. Tracking counts them and shows which way each one is heading.

TensorFlow.js · COCO-SSD

Hears drones

Drones are hard to see but easy to hear. A sound model trained on background noise, drones and birds listens for the buzz.

Teachable Machine audio

Tells the tower

Risky events reach the tower screen in seconds with a spoken alert, an AI-written incident report and a pin on the airport map.

Socket.IO · ElevenLabs · Gemini
Risk levels

Three levels, one clear rule each

The phone scores every moment as green, yellow or red, so the tower only hears about what matters.

The system

How one alert travels

Every alert follows the same seven steps from the runway to the tower. Each service does one job.

What it can’t do yet

  • It works in daylight only. Night would need a thermal camera.
  • The off-the-shelf model works best when birds are reasonably large in the frame.
  • It is an extra set of eyes for the tower, not a replacement for radar.
  1. Camera stationDetect and sendThe phone spots birds or a drone, scores the risk and sends an event with type, count, direction and confidence.
  2. Tiger DataSave the streamEvery event is stored with a timestamp, and hourly summaries feed the tower’s charts.
  3. GeminiVerify with AIFor yellow and red events, Gemini checks the snapshot and writes a short incident report.
  4. MongoDB AtlasStore the reportThe report, snapshot and Gemini’s label are saved together.
  5. Solana devnetStamp drone reportsA hash of each drone report goes on chain, so nobody can quietly edit it later.
  6. ElevenLabsGive it a voiceThe alert sentence becomes a spoken tower announcement.
  7. Socket.IOTell the towerThe alert, report and audio arrive on the tower dashboard instantly.

Questions

What people ask

How accurate is it?

The team measures it on a set of test clips before judging and reports the result, for example how many of 20 clips it caught. Treat it as an extra set of eyes, not a replacement for radar.

Can it see small, far-away birds?

The free model works best when birds are reasonably large in the frame. A zoom lens or a model trained on sky footage would improve that.

Does it work at night?

Not yet. Night detection would need a thermal camera, which is a natural next step.

What about privacy?

The camera points at the sky, and snapshots are saved only when there’s an alert.

Why use a blockchain?

So drone incident records can’t be quietly changed later, which matters if they are used as evidence for police or the FAA.

Isn’t this already done?

Big airports use radar and commercial counter-drone systems. SkySentry is a low-cost version for the small airports that can’t afford those.

What would it cost a real airport?

A phone or cheap camera per runway, plus small cloud bills.
